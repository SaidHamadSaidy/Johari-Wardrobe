A Swahili proverb
Haraka haraka haina baraka.

“Hurry, hurry has no blessing.” That is why every piece is a pre-order: sourced fresh after you order, with your delivery date confirmed personally on WhatsApp.

Ordering

Simple and personal

  1. I.

    Select

    Add your chosen pieces to the bag, with sizes, weights or colours.

  2. II.

    Confirm

    Send your order on WhatsApp or pay by card. We confirm availability, price and delivery date with you.

  3. III.

    Receive

    Pay by M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Mixx by Yas or card, and we deliver to your door.

Delivery

Across Tanzania and beyond

Pre-orders usually ship within 7 to 14 days. We share tracking or rider details on WhatsApp.

Our promise

Returns and exchanges

If your piece arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, message us within 3 days with a photo and we will replace it or refund you. Spices and food cannot be returned once opened.

About
